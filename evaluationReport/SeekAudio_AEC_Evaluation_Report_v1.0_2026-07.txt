Paired conclusions:
  - A1 vs B1 (traditional NS): saves ~54% compute; FE-ST ERLE ~12 dB higher;
    AECMOS FE-ST Echo +0.94, composite +0.37; near-end transparency on par.
    Verdict: A1 leads clearly on compute and echo cancellation, and is better overall in perceptual quality.
  - A2 vs B2 (AI NR): saves ~48% compute (about half of B2); FE-ST ERLE ~12 dB higher.
    B2's NSNet2 is slightly more natural on near-end/double-talk (higher AECMOS NE-ST Other, DT Other),
    but needs ~2x the compute and an extra 1MB flash model partition; A2 reaches comparable overall
    perceptual quality at nearly half the compute.
